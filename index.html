Research

Bridges, towers and other structures reveal their condition through the way they vibrate. I develop models that read those measurements accurately, stay interpretable and respect the underlying physics.

Koopman operator learning

Koopman theory represents nonlinear dynamics through a linear operator acting on observables. I use it for system identification and reduced-order modelling of structural vibration data.

Hybrid physics–data models

Data-driven surrogates can dissipate energy that the real structure conserves, so model error and damage look alike. I couple exact analytical propagators with structure-preserving learned corrections.

Numerical methods for stiff ODEs

My MSc thesis studied block second-derivative methods for stiff ordinary differential equations. A current preprint analyses the stability barriers of these collocation methods.

    A. V. Alagbada and T. Lahmer

    Summary

    A PRISMA-guided review of 67 peer-reviewed studies published between 2010 and 2025. Conference papers

  1. C1

    SAK-Net: Symplectic Attention Koopman Networks for Wave PDEs with Adaptive Analytical-Learned Dynamics In press

    A. V. Alagbada, T. Lahmer, T. Iroko and A. Tchoneteck

    In M. Baeßler, R. Herrmann, F. Hille, E. Niederleithinger and J. F. Unger (eds.), Physics informed Structural Health Monitoring: Proceedings of CSHM-10 Workshop. Lecture Notes in Civil Engineering, vol. 966. Springer, 2026.

    • Proceedings volume
    • Conference talk
    Abstract

    Predicting wave propagation in structures is central to structural health monitoring (SHM), yet many data-driven predictors trade physical consistency for short-horizon accuracy. This conference version introduces SAK-Net, a Symplectic Attention Koopman Network that couples an exact analytical propagator with a structure-preserving learned correction module, and it reports validation on linear and linearly damped wave benchmarks.

    First, we derive a Discrete-Exact Koopman Propagator (DEKP) by matching the leapfrog finite-difference stencil using discrete Laplacian eigenvalues and a backward-difference modal velocity. For undamped linear wave dynamics, the resulting block-diagonal Koopman operator reproduces the generating numerical scheme to machine precision and is symplectic in each 2 × 2 modal phase plane. Second, we formulate and implement a symplectic encoder, symplectic delay-attention, and a symplectic Koopman latent map intended to correct deviations from the analytical model. Third, we implement an adaptive blending mechanism, driven by a physics-based model-mismatch indicator, that selects analytical execution for near-linear regimes and activates learning only when necessary.

    Compared with DMD, EDMD and LSTM baselines, DEKP attains machine-precision errors on the linear benchmark and yields accurate rollouts on linearly damped dynamics while estimating an effective damping rate from modal energy decay. Nonlinear benchmarks, ablations and field-data validation are reserved for the journal extension.
